MEASUREMENT TECHNOLOGY

Dual-energy X-ray Absorptiometry (DXA) is a bone-density measurement technique used to assess Bone Mineral Density (BMD). It helps monitor changes in bone mass during long-duration space missions.

NASA BONE HEALTH RESEARCH

NASA research investigates bone mineral density changes experienced by astronauts during spaceflight. Reduced mechanical loading in microgravity can contribute to bone loss, particularly in weight-bearing regions such as the hip and spine.

Published research has reported approximately 1–2% monthly bone-density loss in the hip and spine in some astronauts. The actual response varies between individuals and missions.
