DATA TRANSPARENCY

Normal and High Stimulation are software profiles based on NASA-published research references. They are not live astronaut measurements. The six-hour profile is a historical research comparison, not an official NASA alarm threshold. The dashboard is not currently connected to a physical Actiwatch or NASA live telemetry. The efficiency and WASO readings remain at the recent in-flight research averages because matching high-condition values are not established by the cited comparison.
