CARDIOVASCULAR MONITORING EQUIPMENT

In a practical astronaut-monitoring system, cardiovascular measurements can be obtained using ECG electrodes and physiological monitoring equipment. ECG signals help determine heart rate and support heart-rate-variability analysis. Blood pressure requires a suitable blood-pressure monitoring instrument.
